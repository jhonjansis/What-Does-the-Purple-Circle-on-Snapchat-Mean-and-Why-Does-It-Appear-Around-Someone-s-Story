What Does the Purple Circle on Snapchat Mean and Why Does It Appear Around Someone’s Story?

Snapchat uses different colours, symbols, and icons to help users understand what is happening in the app. However, some of these symbols can be confusing, especially for people who are new to Snapchat or have recently noticed changes in the interface. One common question is what the purple circle around someone’s Story means and whether it indicates a special type of content.

The purple circle on Snapchat generally indicates that someone has posted a Story you can view. Purple is commonly associated with Snaps and Stories, while other colours and symbols may communicate different types of activity. Understanding these visual indicators can help you navigate Snapchat more confidently and know what to expect when viewing a friend's Story.

In this guide, we will explain the meaning of the purple circle, how it differs from other Snapchat icons, what the purple lock means, and how to understand these symbols when using the app.

What Does the Purple Circle on Snapchat Mean?

The Purple Circle on Snapchat usually appears around a person's Story when they have shared photo or video content that you can watch. You may notice it around a friend's Bitmoji or profile picture in the Stories section.

When you see a purple ring, it generally means there is Story content available to view. Tapping the Story allows you to watch the photos or videos the person has shared.

The exact appearance of Snapchat's interface can vary depending on the app version, where you are viewing the Story, and whether you have already watched it. Therefore, the purple circle should be understood in context rather than as a universal indicator with only one meaning.

It does not automatically mean that someone has sent you a private message, taken a screenshot, or added you to a special list.

Why Does the Purple Ring Snapchat Symbol Appear?

The purple ring Snapchat symbol is associated with Stories and helps users recognise available content. Snapchat uses coloured rings and other visual indicators to make it easier to identify updates from friends.

Here are some common reasons you might notice a purple circle:

1. A Friend Has Posted a Story

The most common explanation is that the person has shared a Story. You can tap their profile picture or Bitmoji to watch it, provided you have permission to view the content.

2. You Are Browsing the Stories Section

The purple ring may be visible while browsing Stories or viewing a friend's profile. Snapchat's interface can display Story indicators differently depending on the section of the app.

3. The Story Has Not Been Viewed Yet

A coloured ring may indicate that Story content is available to watch. However, the precise appearance of the ring can depend on the interface and viewing state. Avoid assuming that every purple circle has exactly the same meaning in every situation.

4. The Person Has Shared New Content

If a friend posts additional Story content, you may notice an updated indicator. Opening the Story is the easiest way to check what has been shared.

What Does the Snapchat Purple Icon Mean?

The Snapchat purple icon can refer to different purple-coloured indicators within the app. Its meaning depends on the icon's shape, location, and surrounding information.

For example, a purple circle around a friend's profile picture is commonly connected with Stories. A purple symbol displayed elsewhere may represent a different feature or type of activity.

It is important to distinguish between a coloured ring, a solid icon, and a lock symbol. Although these indicators may all use purple, they do not necessarily communicate the same information.

If you are unsure about a particular icon, check where it appears. Is it around a Bitmoji, beside a friend's name, or inside a Story preview? The location can help you understand what Snapchat is indicating.

Also, remember that Snapchat regularly updates its interface. An icon's appearance may change slightly between versions, so keeping the app updated can help you see the latest design.

What Does the Purple Lock on Snapchat Mean?

The purple lock on Snapchat is commonly associated with a Private Story. Private Stories allow users to choose which friends can view particular Story content.

Unlike a regular Story that may be available to a broader audience according to the user's privacy settings, a Private Story is intended for a selected group of people.

What the purple lock generally indicates:
  • Selected audience: The person has chosen specific friends who can view the Private Story.
  • Limited visibility: Not everyone on their friends list necessarily has access to that Story.
  • Personal sharing: The feature gives users greater control over who sees certain photos and videos.
  • Separate Story content: A person may share a Private Story alongside their regular Story.

Seeing a lock symbol does not mean that you can automatically access every Private Story belonging to that person. You must be included in the audience selected by the person who created it.

The lock symbol and the purple Story ring are therefore different indicators. The ring commonly relates to available Story content, while the lock identifies a privacy-related Story feature.

Purple Circle vs Purple Lock on Snapchat

Although these symbols may look similar, their meanings are different.

These meanings can vary slightly with Snapchat's interface and updates. Always consider the symbol's position and the type of content displayed.

Does the Purple Circle Mean Someone Added You to Their Private Story?

No, not necessarily. A purple circle alone does not confirm that someone has added you to a Private Story.

If you see a lock symbol associated with a Story, it may indicate that the content is a Private Story. However, the coloured ring by itself should not be treated as proof that you have been specifically selected for private viewing.

The best way to understand the difference is to look at the full Story indicator. If a lock appears, it suggests a privacy-restricted Story. If you see only a regular purple ring, it is generally associated with available Story content.

Can You Remove the Purple Circle on Snapchat?

You usually do not need to remove the purple circle manually. It is a visual indicator associated with Story content, rather than a separate setting that must be disabled.

If you want to stop seeing a particular Story, you can review Snapchat's available Story and notification controls. Depending on the feature and app version, you may be able to mute a friend's Stories or adjust relevant notification settings.

Watching a Story may also change its viewing indicator. However, the exact appearance after viewing can vary, so the purple ring should not be relied upon as a precise record of whether someone has watched your content.

If the icon looks unusual or does not behave as expected, try updating Snapchat and reopening the app.

How to Understand Snapchat Story Symbols

Snapchat uses several symbols to make its features easier to recognise. Learning their general meanings can help you avoid confusing ordinary Stories with private content.

  1. Check the location: An icon around a profile picture may mean something different from an icon beside a message.
  2. Look for a lock: A lock commonly signals a Private Story or restricted audience.
  3. Tap the Story: If you have permission to view it, opening the Story can help you identify the content.
  4. Review privacy settings: Snapchat's privacy options determine who can see the content a user shares.
  5. Keep the app updated: Interface changes can affect how symbols appear.

It is also important not to assume that the purple circle indicates someone's online status. Snapchat has separate indicators for certain activity and presence-related features.

The purple circle on Snapchat is generally associated with Stories that are available to view. It helps users recognise Story content shared by friends, although its exact appearance can depend on the part of the app you are using.
